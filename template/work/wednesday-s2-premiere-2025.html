Overview

London world premiere of Wednesday Season 2. Vogue UK documented the beauty concept in detail.

Beauty direction

Mélanie Inglessis aimed for balance between editorial and red carpet: bleached brows carried from another project, monochromatic skin (Barbara Sturm Glow Drops, Dior Forever Skin Foundation), intensely carved cheekbones (Laduora Lumeo), no blush, and a dark lip with Dior Rouge Contour #400. Hair by Cesar Deleon Ramirez.
